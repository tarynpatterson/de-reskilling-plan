# Certification roadmap

Target two certifications: AWS Data Engineer Associate around month 4, then DP-700 or SnowPro Core around month 6. Fees and exam codes below are approximate and change often, so verify on each vendor's site before paying.

| Priority | Certification | Approx. fee | Free prep modules | Target date |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AWS Certified Data Engineer - Associate (DEA-C01) | $150 | AWS Skill Builder free exam prep; official exam guide and sample questions | End of week 16 |
| 2 | Microsoft Certified: Fabric Data Engineer Associate (DP-700) | $165 | Microsoft Learn DP-700 learning paths; free practice assessment | End of week 24 |
| 2 (alt) | SnowPro Core | $175 | Snowflake University free courses; Snowflake docs | End of week 24 |
| 3 | Databricks Certified Data Engineer Associate | $200 | Databricks Academy free courses | Optional, after week 24 |
| 4 | Google Professional Data Engineer | $200 | Google Cloud Skills Boost free labs | Optional |
| 5 | HashiCorp Terraform Associate | $70 | HashiCorp tutorials | Optional, cheap IaC signal |
| 6 | dbt Analytics Engineering | $200 | dbt Learn | Skip unless a job requires it |

(For the Analytics Engineering track's certification plan, see `plan/ae-track.md`.)

## AWS DEA-C01 study plan (weeks 11-16)

1. Week 11: read the exam guide and note the four domains (ingestion and transformation, data store management, operations and support, security and governance). Score yourself per domain.
2. Weeks 11-13: follow the Phase 3 labs. After each lab, write 5 flashcards on what the service does, when to pick it, and its limits.
3. Week 14: take one full practice exam (Tutorials Dojo). Log every wrong answer with the concept behind it.
4. Week 15 (shared with Azure study): redo labs for your two weakest domains and retake a second practice exam. Aim for 80%+ before booking.
5. Week 16: book the exam. Review flashcards daily, do a final timed practice exam 2 days before, and sit the exam.

## DP-700 or SnowPro Core study plan (weeks 21-24)

1. Choose based on target employers. Pick DP-700 if job postings you want mention Azure or Fabric; pick SnowPro Core if they mention Snowflake.
2. Week 21: complete the free vendor learning path outline and note gaps.
3. Weeks 22-23: do the vendor practice assessment, then hands-on labs for weak areas.
4. Week 24: book the exam, take two timed practice runs, and sit it.

## Cost-saving tips

- [ ] Ask your employer about a training voucher or reimbursement before paying
- [ ] Watch for AWS and Microsoft free-voucher or discount events
- [ ] Buy paid prep courses only during sales; use the free official material first
